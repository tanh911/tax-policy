**Last Updated: November 16, 2026**

TaxCheck VN ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Data Provided by Users
- **Search Queries & Lookups**: We process the Vietnamese tax codes (Mã Số Thuế - MST) that you explicitly enter, paste, or highlight on web pages to query business records.
- **License Keys**: If you upgrade to TaxCheck VN PRO, your license key is stored locally in your browser's storage to unlock premium capabilities.
- **Payment Information**: We do **not** collect or store your payment details (credit cards, banking info). All transactions are securely processed by our third-party Merchant of Record (**Lemon Squeezy**).

### 1.2 Automatically Handled Data
- **Public Business Data**: The extension queries public enterprise registries via verified API endpoints (such as `api.vietqr.io`) to retrieve company names, addresses, and official legal operating statuses.
- **Local Cache**: To optimize query performance and reduce network latency, lookup results are cached locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) for 24 hours. No personal browsing history or identity data is collected or transmitted.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To look up company registration and legal operating statuses.
- To detect and warn users about inactive, suspended, or high-risk business tax codes.
- To enable bulk invoice checking and CSV export features for accounting reconciliation.
- To verify your PRO license activation state.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Local Client-Side Storage**: All user preferences, lookup history, local cache, and license keys are stored solely in your browser's local storage.
- **No Cloud Tracking**: We do not maintain user tracking databases or transmit personal logs to external cloud storage.
- **Data Encryption**: All communication with public business data APIs is encrypted via standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

## 4. Third-Party Access
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties. The extension only sends tax code numbers to public business directory endpoints to fetch public legal statuses.

## 5. Permissions Used
- **contextMenus**: To display a right-click menu option allowing quick tax code checks on selected text.
- **storage**: To save your local preferences, search history, and license key on your machine.
- **activeTab**: To detect highlighted tax code numbers and display the inline result popup on the current page without tracking browsing activity.

## 6. Your Rights
You can clear your entire search history at any time directly within the extension popup by clicking "Xóa lịch sử", or by uninstalling the extension from your browser.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **supportsplitvie@gmail.com**
